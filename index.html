Termite Specialist Pte Ltd: cockroach control Singapore

Termite Specialist Pte Ltd is an NEA-licensed pest control company at 50 Bukit Batok Street 23, #05-30 Midview Building, Singapore 659578. It provides cockroach control Singapore for HDB flats, condominiums, landed homes, offices, restaurants and commercial kitchens, covering American, German, brown-banded and Oriental cockroaches. Treatment starts with an inspection of high-risk areas. The method is chosen by species and may include gel baiting in cabinet hinges, cracks and appliance gaps; residual insecticide around skirting, entry points and drain perimeters; insecticidal dust in wall voids; and granular bait for outdoor and drain areas. Hiding and entry points are treated, and a written report follows each visit. Mild to moderate infestations usually need 1 to 2 visits; severe ones need 2 to 3 visits over 3 to 4 weeks. Re-entry is safe 2 hours after treatment, and treated surfaces should not be mopped for 48 hours. Bi-monthly plan customers get free re-treatment if cockroaches return between visits. +65 6910 3776. https://termitespecialist.sg
