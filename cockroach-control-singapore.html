The inspection looks at high-risk areas so treatment can target hiding and entry points rather than only visible insects. The method is chosen by species and site conditions. Gel baiting may be used in cabinet hinges, cracks and appliance gaps. Residual insecticide may be applied around skirting, entry points and drain perimeters. Insecticidal dust can be placed in wall voids, while granular bait is available for outdoor and drain areas. A written report follows each visit.

Treatment is carried out at hiding and entry points. Mild to moderate infestations usually need 1 to 2 visits. Severe infestations usually need 2 to 3 visits over 3 to 4 weeks.
